# Glossary

**Thought**: A single line the visitor writes about something weighing on their mind, in order to let go of it. Never stored; it disappears when its Ritual ends.
_Avoid_: wish, deseo

**Place**: A selectable setting the visitor sits in (currently the forest campfire and the beach). A Place defines its scenery, its ambient sounds, and the Ritual applied to a Thought.
_Avoid_: background, fondo, scene

**Ritual**: What a Place does with a released Thought: the forest burns it in the fire; the beach seals it in a bottle and lets the sea carry it away.
_Avoid_: animation

**Weather**: The sky condition layered over any Place: clear, rain, or snow. Independent of the Place; rain has its own sound, snow is silent.

**Closing line**: The short consoling sentence shown when a Ritual ends (e.g. "the sea took it"). Each Place has its own set.
_Avoid_: note, release note

**Presence**: The other visitors who have the page open at the same moment. Their released Thoughts may appear as passing stars; nothing is stored.
_Avoid_: room, peers
